Credit Rating Advisory

Now more than ever, credit rating plays a crucial role for company growth. A good rating entails higher credibility and results in lower interest rates. Backed by domain expertise, we prepare clients thoroughly for rating assessments.

Financial Restructuring

Profit and loss are part and parcel of business. We ensure losses are only temporary hurdles by providing comprehensive loan restructuring services to stressed businesses—be it tenure extension or interest rate reduction.

Insolvency Services
